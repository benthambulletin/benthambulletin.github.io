The bottom line

  1. Democrats are favored to take the HouseGeneric ballot D+8.9 (Silver Bulletin, Oct 9). Cook projects a Democratic gain of 9–19 seats.
  2. The Senate is a coin flip tilting DemocraticDems need net +4. Five GOP-held seats are toss-ups: Maine, Ohio, Texas, Iowa, Kansas. Markets: ~63% Democratic Senate.
  3. Trump is deeply underwaterNet approval −25 across trackers; Pew has approval at 30%, the lowest of either term.
  4. Hochul is comfortably ahead in New YorkUp 11–12 on Blakeman (Marist, Emerson), though Blakeman leads Upstate.
  5. Buffalo and Long Island House seats are safe; NY-17 isn'tNY-26 (Kennedy) Solid D; NY-01 (LaLota) Lean R, no poll. Lawler vs Conley in the Hudson Valley is the state's only toss-up: Conley 48–46.
  6. North Tonawanda: Ortt vs Arida and an $18M bondState Senate 62 and the North Tonawanda community center bond are the two local items to watch. Early voting Oct 24–Nov 1.
