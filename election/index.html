The bottom line

  1. Democrats are favored to take the HouseGeneric ballot D+8.9 (Silver Bulletin, Oct 9). Cook projects a Democratic gain of 9–19 seats.
  2. The Senate is a coin flip tilting DemocraticDems need net +4. Five GOP-held seats are toss-ups: Maine, Ohio, Texas, Iowa, Kansas. Markets: ~63% Democratic Senate.
  3. Trump is deeply underwaterNet approval −25 across trackers; Pew has approval at 30%, the lowest of either term.
  4. Hochul is comfortably ahead in New YorkUp 11–12 on Blakeman (Marist, Emerson), though Blakeman leads Upstate.
  5. Buffalo and Long Island House seats are safe; NY-17 isn'tNY-26 (Kennedy) Solid D; NY-01 (LaLota) Lean R, no poll. Lawler vs Conley in the Hudson Valley is the state's only toss-up: Conley 48–46.
  6. Your ballot: five towns, one box at the bottomNorth Tonawanda, Orchard Park, Scottsville, Dunkirk and Fredonia — Congress, state legislature, county seats and the one proposition (NT's $18M community center). Early voting Oct 24–Nov 1.

What changed since yesterday

  • Texas: two new polls Oct 14 keep Talarico ahead of Paxton (Marist 47–45, UT 50–47). Still the hardest of the Democrats' five pickups, but it has not slipped.
  • Maine: Suffolk (Oct 8–11) has Jackson 49–47 over Collins, the third straight poll with Jackson ahead by 2–4. Maine stays the likeliest flip; Collins beat her 2020 polls by 12, so treat a 2-point lead as even.
  • Iowa: YouGov (Oct 14) Turek 47–46, after Emerson and CNN had Hinson up 2. A true coin flip, and the plus-four math needs it or Kansas.
  • Kansas: no new poll since NYT/Siena's 47–46 Marshall; Cook's Oct 6 move to Toss Up stands. Sabato still lists five toss-ups, all Republican-held: Kansas, Iowa, Maine, Ohio, Texas.
  • Holds: North Carolina (Cooper +14) and Georgia (Ossoff +8) look safe; no new Michigan poll. If those hold, Democrats need four of Maine, Ohio, Texas, Iowa, Kansas, Alaska.
