The National Wire

The Lead

Macy’s Parade Postponed, a First

For the first time in its history, the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed because of weather. The 100th parade will now step off Saturday at 8:30 a.m., Macy’s said Wednesday, as a powerful coastal storm moves into New York.

The nor’easter is expected to make it too windy for the balloons to fly safely, the BBC reports. The parade was suspended during World War Two, but it has never been put off for weather; in 1971 Macy’s kept the balloons on the ground instead. Governor Kathy Hochul declared a state of emergency Wednesday night for Long Island, New York City and Westchester County.

Macy’s had planned 100 balloons for the 100th parade, including 10 new ones: Bluey in a crown and a Minion riding a banana like a horse.

“While we are heartbroken that our parade was postponed, the safety of our participants and spectators is and always will be our top priority.” — Will Coss, the parade’s executive producer

Steelers Cut Aaron Rodgers

The Pittsburgh Steelers released Aaron Rodgers, the 42-year-old four-time MVP, on Wednesday, shortly after he tore into coach Mike McCarthy on ESPN’s “The Pat McAfee Show,” NPR reports. It capped about 18 hours that began with the team benching him for backup Mason Rudolph ahead of Friday’s game against Denver; McCarthy said Rodgers had given him an “ultimatum” on Tuesday.

3

A Million SpaceX Satellites Approved

The Federal Communications Commission has approved SpaceX’s plan to launch a million satellites that would run on sunlight and process data for AI in orbit, the first approval of its kind, the BBC reports. Amazon called it a “lofty ambition rather than a real plan,” and U.S. astronomers said it would “significantly undermine” the public’s ability to study the night sky.

North Tonawanda · Oliver Street Rallies After Two Fires

Two fires on Nov. 17 in the same Oliver Street building, one around 3 a.m. and another that evening, destroyed Academy Auction House and Carl’s Tattooing, WKBW reports; the fire department called the first one accidental and electrical, and nobody was hurt in either. Neighbors have set up GoFundMe campaigns totaling more than $10,000 for owners Matt Hoffman and Carl Cederman, with others for families who lived upstairs.

Andy Taylor of Duran Duran Dies at 65

Andy Taylor, Duran Duran’s original guitarist on “Hungry Like the Wolf,” “Rio” and “The Reflex,” hits tied up with the launch of MTV, has died at 65, his family and bandmates said, NPR reports. The band said he brought “an indelible fire and swagger to our music.”

U2 Plays the First Thanksgiving Eve Game

U2 headlined halftime of the NFL’s first Thanksgiving Eve game, Packers at Rams, streamed on Netflix, opening with “Sunday Bloody Sunday” and paying tribute to Dolly Parton, Billboard reports. The tribute was “Torn,” her duet with Bono and the final vocal recording of Parton’s career, Rolling Stone reports.

On This Day

November 26, 1789 — The country kept its first national Thanksgiving, a Thursday set by George Washington in a proclamation dated October 3, after the House asked that a committee “wait upon the President” about a day of thanks. It didn’t become a yearly habit. Abraham Lincoln brought it back in 1863, following Washington’s example down to the date: his proclamation also came on October 3, and his Thanksgiving also fell on Thursday, November 26. Congress didn’t pin it to the fourth Thursday until 1941, after a public outcry over Franklin Roosevelt’s attempt to move it up a week to stretch the Christmas shopping season. This year the fourth Thursday is November 26 again.
