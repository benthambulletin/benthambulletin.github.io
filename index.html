☾On This Day☾

September 6, 1901 — 125 years ago this afternoon, President McKinley was shaking hands in the Temple of Music at Buffalo's Pan-American Exposition when Leon Czolgosz reached the front of the line with a pistol under a bandaged hand and fired twice. McKinley died eight days later. Theodore Roosevelt took the oath in a house on Delaware Avenue, two miles from the spot.
