Economics Nobel to Two Americans

Robert E. Hall and Michael Woodford won for their work on how to smooth out boom-and-bust cycles to limit unemployment and inflation. Their models help governments and central banks respond to economic shocks.

2

Trump Announces an "Energy Ceasefire"

The president said Russia and Ukraine agreed to stop hitting each other's power plants but gave no details. Zelensky said he would back it if Moscow does, while saying Russian strikes on power plants continue.

3

139 Dead as Migrant Boat Capsizes

The boat, carrying 305 people from Djibouti to Yemen, overturned near the port town of Obock; children are among the dead, the U.N. says. Dozens are still missing.

4

Tropical Storm Rachel Soaks California

The storm flooded parts of the Long Beach waterfront before weakening and moving inland. Forecasters call it a preview of a stormy season for the state.
