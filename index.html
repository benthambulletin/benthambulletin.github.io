✶The National Wire✶

1

Deadly Train Crash in Spain

A high-speed train slammed into the back of another that had stopped in Toledo province, in central Spain, this morning, derailing two cars and killing at least 30 people, regional officials told AFP, CBS reports. Both trains were running from Madrid toward Seville; the cause isn't known yet.

2

Update: Israel's Vote Ends in a Stalemate

Neither Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's coalition nor Gadi Eisenkot's opposition bloc won a majority, exit polls show, CBS reports. With 70 percent counted, Netanyahu's Likud has 20.6 percent and Eisenkot's Yashar 15.9; whoever governs needs a majority of the Knesset's 120 seats.

3

Methane Limits Rolled Back

President Trump said Tuesday he is "terminating" a Biden-era rule limiting methane, a planet-warming gas, from oil and gas drilling, NPR reports. He said the change would unlock 210 million barrels of oil, which NPR notes is about 10 days of U.S. use.

4

The Rays Stay Alive

Tampa Bay beat Milwaukee 4–3 Tuesday night to win Game 4 of the World Series. The Brewers still lead three games to one, and Game 5 is tonight at 8 in Tampa Bay.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Rochester · Orangutans Are Coming Back

Seneca Park Zoo broke ground Tuesday on its $135 million Tropics exhibit, WXXI and Rochester First report. Orangutans and lemurs, gone from the zoo since 2018, will live there when it opens in late 2029.

2

Town of Niagara · Neighbors Ask to Move for Good

Families near Gill Creek in the Town of Niagara, where state and federal testing found dangerous radiation levels, are asking to be relocated permanently, not just housed temporarily, WKBW reports. The EPA moved some families whose results came back high, but a few dozen neighbors around them were never moved.

3

Fredonia · Boil Your Water

Fredonia is under a boil-water order for "high turbidity levels and treatment deficiencies," meaning cloudy water the plant couldn't fully treat, the Observer reports. The village bought 966,000 gallons from Dunkirk over the weekend; boil water for at least a minute before drinking, cooking or brushing teeth.

★The Marquee★

1

Horror Night on Dancing With the Stars

Dancing With the Stars went horror for Halloween week, with routines to music from Sinners, Black Swan and Joker, the Hollywood Reporter reports. The night brought the season's first perfect score and a double elimination; Jackson Olson and Tyler Cameron went home.

2

Demi Lovato Says She's Fine

Demi Lovato says she's "fine" after a reported brief hospital visit for an allergic reaction Tuesday, Billboard reports, citing People. In a since-deleted post, she guessed she's newly allergic to passionfruit or sushi.

3

Foo Fighters Take Over Kimmel

Foo Fighters are playing four nights on Jimmy Kimmel Live! for the show's Halloween week, Billboard reports. Monday they played "Window" from their 12th album, Your Favorite Toy; Tuesday they reached back to "This Is a Call," from their first album in 1995.
