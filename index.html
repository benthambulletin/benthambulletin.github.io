🍂The Turning🍂

The state's foliage report starts today. For forty years I LOVE NY has sent volunteer spotters out every week to log how far the leaves have turned, and the first of this season's reports lands this afternoon; new ones come every Wednesday until the trees are bare. Niagara County covers North Tonawanda, Chautauqua County covers Dunkirk, Monroe County covers Scottsville.
Numbers here starting tomorrow, refreshed every Wednesday. Nothing has turned yet to speak of — look at the tops of the maples on a back road and you will see the first yellow. North Tonawanda usually peaks in the last week of October.
