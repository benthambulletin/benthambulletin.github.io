✶The National Wire✶

1

Israel Votes

Israelis are voting today in their first national election since the Oct. 7, 2023, attack, and turnout is the highest since 1981, the BBC reports. Polls show Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu nearly even with former military chief Gadi Eisenkot, but neither side is projected to reach the 61 seats needed to govern, so weeks of coalition bargaining may follow.

2

Update: The Oleshky Truce Begins

The ceasefire in Oleshky, the Russian-occupied Ukrainian city cut off from food, water and electricity, has started, and both Ukrainian officials and Russia's defense ministry say it is holding, the BBC reports. Aid deliveries and evacuations are set for Friday; residents told the BBC they have been cooking garden weeds to survive.

3

Dollar General Sued Over Prices

The Federal Trade Commission and 20 states sued Dollar General Monday, accusing the chain of charging more at the register than its shelf prices said, for years, CBS reports. The FTC says the mislabeling took "millions of dollars in unlawful overcharges" from shoppers at its 21,000 stores.

4

Comey Charged a Third Time

Federal prosecutors moved to charge former FBI Director James Comey with leaking national defense information, the third time the Justice Department has tried to prosecute him, the BBC reports. His lawyers say the case involves an alleged 2016 disclosure to the New York Times; Comey calls it an "appalling abuse."

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Rochester · The School for the Deaf Turns 150

The Rochester School for the Deaf on St. Paul Street is marking 150 years since it opened, and alumni came back this month to meet today's students, WXXI reports. "RSD is really my second home," said Patti Canne, class of 1981, now a social worker at the University of Rochester, speaking in American Sign Language through an interpreter.

2

Niagara Falls · Water Rates Could Rise 3%

The Niagara Falls Water Board is weighing a 3 percent rate increase for 2027, the Niagara Gazette reports. The proposed budget is just over $39.8 million, up from $39.28 million; the board's director says costs are under control but revenue is falling.

3

Dunkirk · Perch and Walleye Are Biting

Yellow perch fishing on Lake Erie is "kicking into high gear" in 50 to 55 feet of water between Cattaraugus Creek and Sturgeon Point, the Observer's outdoors column says. Walleye are schooled up at 80 to 110 feet from Dunkirk west to Barcelona, and one three-man boat limited out off Dunkirk in two hours.

1

Jay-Z Gets the Super Bowl

Jay-Z will headline the Super Bowl halftime show at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles on Valentine's Day, announced during Monday Night Football, the Hollywood Reporter reports. He follows Bad Bunny, whose show at this year's Super Bowl won an Emmy.

2

Taylor Swift Joins the Songwriters

Taylor Swift was inducted into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame Monday night, with Brad Paisley doing the honors, Billboard reports. The 14-time Grammy winner told the room the writers of Music Row "raised me."

3

Noah Wyle, Sexiest Man Alive

People named Noah Wyle its Sexiest Man Alive for 2026, his first time, Variety reports. He has been a TV doctor since 1994, when he started as John Carter on ER.
