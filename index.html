✶The National Wire✶

1

Brewers Win It All

Milwaukee beat Tampa Bay 4–1 in Game 5 Wednesday night to win the World Series, the first championship in the franchise's history, CBS reports. The Brewers took the series four games to one.

2

Update: Spain's Toll Rises to 69

At least 69 people died when a state-run high-speed train ran into the back of a stopped train near Urda, south of Madrid, Wednesday morning, the BBC reports. Audio published in Spanish media appears to show the moving train was cleared to go even though the other had stalled about 15 miles ahead; it is Spain's second deadly train crash this year, NPR notes.

3

DoorDash Buys Instacart

DoorDash said this morning it will buy Instacart for $17 billion, or $65 a share, uniting a major restaurant-delivery company with one of the nation's top grocery-delivery services, CBS reports. DoorDash says more supermarkets will be able to use Instacart's tools to run their own websites.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Rochester · A Memorial for First Responders

Monroe County dedicated a new First Responders Memorial on Civic Center Plaza Wednesday, WXXI reports. It stands next to the memorials for fallen sheriff's deputies and city police and firefighters.

2

Lockport · Atomic-Era Mill Cleanup Gets Its Contractor

The Army Corps of Engineers awarded a $249 million contract to Sevenson Environmental to clean up the old Guterl Specialty Steel site off Ohio Street, the Niagara Gazette reports. Nine buildings in the former Simonds Saw & Steel complex handled uranium for the atomic program after World War II; work is expected to start next year.

3

Fredonia · Volleyball Rolls On

Fredonia's girls swept Salamanca 25–21, 25–3, 25–15 Wednesday in the Section VI Class B2 volleyball quarterfinals at Fredonia High, the Observer reports. Tied 20–20 in the first set, the 'Billies scored five of the next six points to take it.

★The Marquee★

1

Paddington Stays in London

Paddington the Musical has extended its West End run through June 2028, Variety reports. It tied the record with seven Olivier Awards this year and nears its first anniversary at the Savoy Theatre.

2

U2, Up Close

U2 will play an intimate show for a select group of fans for BBC Music in central London on Nov. 3, Billboard reports. The set mixes classics with songs from the coming album Carnaval de Luz and airs on BBC Two later in November.

3

Prada Goes Hand-Drawn

Prada's new holiday campaign starring Sadie Sink and Maya Hawke was drawn by hand by veteran Disney animator John Pomeroy, Variety reports. It has drawn wide praise online since its debut Wednesday night, at a time when AI is everywhere in advertising.
