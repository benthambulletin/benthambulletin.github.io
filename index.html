The Lead

No Bill Will Wear 83 Again

The Buffalo Bills will retire Andre Reed's number, 83, during the 2027 season, the team announced Thursday, the Associated Press reports in the Dunkirk Observer. Retiring a number means no Bills player will be issued it again.

The team shared a video of owner Terry Pegula breaking the news to Reed in person. "I can still see you going over the middle, getting knocked around, hanging onto the ball," Pegula told him. Jim Kelly and Thurman Thomas, his old teammates and fellow Hall of Famers, walked in wearing copies of his jersey.

Reed, a wide receiver, played 15 of his 16 seasons in Buffalo and was a mainstay of the teams that went to four straight Super Bowls in the 1990s. He finished with 951 catches for 13,198 yards and went into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2014. The team has not yet said which game next season will hold the ceremony, Buffalo Rumblings reports.

Fiona the Hippo Has Died

Fiona, the Cincinnati Zoo hippopotamus who became famous after her premature birth in 2017, died Thursday, days after swallowing a knit hat that landed in her enclosure, CBS reports. The zoo said a visitor had tossed the hat in the air and a ceiling fan flung it in; she would have turned 10 in January.

2

A Pardon for Pete Rose

President Trump on Thursday pardoned Pete Rose, baseball's career hits leader, who died in 2024 at 83, the BBC reports. Rose pleaded guilty in 1990 to filing false tax returns and was banned from baseball for betting on games; the league lifted that ban last year, which made him eligible for the Hall of Fame.

3

The Army Puts Off a Risky Blast in Iowa

The Army has delayed until Monday its plan to blow up unstable explosives at an ammunition plant in Middletown, Iowa, CBS reports; it had been set for noon today. Elected officials and environmental groups had warned that the blast could send a cloud of dust carrying toxic lead across parts of Iowa and Illinois.

The Home Wire

1

Finger Lakes · A Measles Warning in Geneva

Anyone who visited Geneva General Hospital between 11:30 a.m. Monday and 4:45 p.m. Tuesday may have been exposed to measles, WXXI reports, after a patient there tested positive. Ontario County health officials say those visitors should check their vaccination records and watch for symptoms through Dec. 8.

2

Fredonia · SUNY Grows, but Not Here

Enrollment across the state university system rose for the fourth year in a row, to 392,401 students, but SUNY Fredonia and Buffalo State are still shrinking, WKBW reports. "I continue to worry about Buffalo State and Fredonia," Chancellor John King told the station.

3

Niagara Falls · Tips by Text, No Name Needed

Niagara Falls police have set up an anonymous tip line, WKBW reports: anyone can text 716-804-4000 without giving a name. Police say it is meant for people who know something that could help a case but are afraid to come forward.

The Marquee

1

Grand Theft Auto 6 Arrives, and Breaks Records

The video game Grand Theft Auto 6 came out Thursday after a 13-year wait and is estimated to be the biggest entertainment launch in history, the BBC reports. Advance orders and first-day sales came to more than 24.5 million copies, about $2.36 billion, by the count of the research firm Sensor Tower.

2

A New Hunger Games Opens Today

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, a prequel taken from Suzanne Collins's novel of last year, opens in 3,900 North American theaters today, the Hollywood Reporter says. The studio projects more than $80 million for the weekend, and the trade paper's sources expect it to pass $100 million.

3

Martin Short and the Wedding Cake

On Jimmy Kimmel Live, Martin Short admitted he took a fork to a cake at Selena Gomez's wedding, thinking each group of guests had its own; it was the wedding cake, Rolling Stone reports. "I love the teeth marks of the fork where he tried to save it," Gomez said.

The Gridiron

Bills: Four Sit Out Thursday

Cornerback Christian Benford, tight end Dalton Kincaid, linebacker Dorian Williams and safety Damar Hamlin did not practice Thursday, Buffalo Rumblings reports, three days before the Dolphins come to Orchard Park. Cornerback Dee Alford, coming back from a concussion, moved up to limited work, and guard O'Cyrus Torrence returned.

Jets: Smith Plans to Start

Quarterback Geno Smith said the plan is for him to start Sunday against the Chargers in Los Angeles, Gang Green Nation reports, though ESPN's Rich Cimini noted he was walking with a limp on Wednesday.

Thursday Night: Texans 26, Colts 3

C.J. Stroud threw three touchdown passes and Houston outgained Indianapolis 355 yards to 167, ProFootballTalk reports.
