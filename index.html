✶The National Wire✶

The Lead

Nvidia Investigates Its Own Boss

Nvidia, the company whose chips power the artificial-intelligence boom, has opened an independent investigation of its co-founder and CEO, Jensen Huang, CBS reports. It follows New York Times reports that he had a close relationship with Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman U.S. intelligence agencies believe worked for China's Ministry of State Security, its main spy agency. The two are not related.

In a message to employees Sunday, Huang admitted being "unfaithful" to his wife and apologized to her and their children, without naming the woman. "There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry," he wrote.

Nvidia's board says it has "no reason to believe" national security or company secrets were compromised, NPR reports, but that its inquiry "will examine all available information." Huang has led the company for 30 years, the BBC notes.

Update: Only Kidding, Dana

President Trump said Sunday he "was only kidding" when he offered UFC boss Dana White the job of White House press secretary, the BBC reports. "Dana loves the idea," he said, adding that White would visit him Monday "for other reasons."

Nine Hours Treading Water

Two fishermen found a cruise ship pianist treading water about 20 miles off the California coast near Catalina Island on Friday, after he fell overboard from Royal Caribbean's Ovation of the Seas, the BBC and CBS report; officials think he was in the Pacific for nine hours. "The only thing he said to me was, 'Thank you. I thought I was gonna die,'" fisherman Sean Norton said.

Rochester · A Local Man Gets the Mariners

Jason Kanzler, a Rochester-area native and former University at Buffalo baseball player, is the new manager of the Seattle Mariners, the club announced, WGRZ reports.

Dunkirk · A New Fire Contract

The Common Council unanimously approved a fire department contract that runs through Jan. 1, 2029, with no raises in 2025 and 2026, then 4 percent in 2027 and 2 percent in 2028, the Observer reports. It adds an emergency medical training officer and drops the assistant chief's position.

3

Westfield · School Work 57 Percent Done

Westfield Academy and Central School's building project is about 57 percent complete, with new locker rooms due this month, the Observer reports. The board approved a new playground, though one member said the district was being "overcharged" at more than half a million dollars.

Taylor Swift and her longtime friend Karlie Kloss sat together in the VIP section at Arrowhead on Sunday to watch Travis Kelce's Chiefs play the Jets, Billboard and Page Six report. Fans who had followed rumors of a falling-out called it "war is over."

Ariana, De Niro and Stiller Sing

Ariana Grande and her Focker-in-Law co-stars Robert De Niro and Ben Stiller sang "O Christmas Tree" on Sunday's Tonight Show, Billboard reports, as Jimmy Fallon marked the arrival of the Rockefeller Center Christmas tree. Grande took the high notes; Stiller gave "a valiant effort."

Justin Timberlake's Family Runway

Justin Timberlake walked the runway with his sons, Silas, 11, and Phineas, 6, and his niece Zaya at the Global Down Syndrome Foundation's fashion show in Denver on Saturday, Us Weekly reports. The fundraiser brought in more than $2.7 million for research.
