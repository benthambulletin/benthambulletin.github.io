The Weekend · Margo

Saturday’s the one. The showers quit by 1 in North Tonawanda and Dunkirk, it gets up to 47 and 48, and a west wind gusting to 26 reminds you it’s still November. Sunday warms to 51, 53 in Scottsville, and stays dry till afternoon; then the rain comes back Sunday night, 100 percent, and it means it. So: lights up Saturday afternoon, leftovers Sunday, and if you’re driving home from somewhere, leave before dinner. Not one f‑‑‑ing snowflake in the forecast. — Margo
