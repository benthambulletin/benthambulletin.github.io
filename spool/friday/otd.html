On This Day

November 27, 1924 — Macy’s held its first Thanksgiving parade, at 9 a.m., from West 145th Street and Convent Avenue down six miles of Manhattan to its Herald Square store. It was called the Macy’s Christmas Parade, and it had Mother Goose floats to match the store’s Christmas windows, costumed marchers, Santa Claus and animals borrowed from the Central Park Zoo, with about 10,000 people watching. The balloons didn’t come until 1927. Within days Macy’s announced it would do it again the next year, and the 100th one, put off by weather for the first time, steps off Saturday.
