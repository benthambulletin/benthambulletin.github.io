☾On This Day☾

September 7, 1813 — a Troy, New York newspaper printed the earliest known use of “Uncle Sam” for the United States, borrowed from Sam Wilson, a local meatpacker stamping barrels “U.S.” for the Army during the War of 1812. He is buried in Troy.
