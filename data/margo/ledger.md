# Margo's ledger

What the family has said to the paper (Ask Margo questions, family-form entries, Sunday answers, anything Garret passes along for print), one line each, newest at the bottom. Margo reads this before every answer and uses it against them. Only things said for print; never the migraine log, health, money or anything private.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD · Name · what they said (short) · where it ran`
