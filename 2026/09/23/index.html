🍂The Turning🍂

The first numbers are in, and they are close to zero. The state's opening report has no significant change at the Chautauqua Institution, which is the Dunkirk beat; no significant change in Greece, which stands in for Monroe County and Scottsville; and only a trace near Niagara Falls — the first color logged anywhere in Niagara County. Five percent or less in Geneseo, for scale. A new report lands this afternoon.
The Adirondacks are already at thirty-five percent, which is how this always goes — the color starts in the mountains and walks south and west. Chautauqua-Allegheny usually peaks in mid-October and North Tonawanda in the last week. This week's cold nights and hard sun are what push it along.
