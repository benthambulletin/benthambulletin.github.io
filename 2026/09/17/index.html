Pomfret · Fire Destroys the White Village

A call came in just after 12:15 Wednesday afternoon with flames already through the roof at 5362 Route 5, west of Dunkirk. Fredonia Chief Joshua Myers said crews found fire on the first floor that had reached the second. Route 5 was closed westbound; smoke was thick enough that the building could not be seen from the far side of the road. Getting water to the site was a problem. Nine departments worked it — Fredonia, Dunkirk, West Dunkirk, East Dunkirk, Sunset Bay, Stockton, Brocton, Sheridan and county emergency services. The restaurant had been closed; the building is a loss.
