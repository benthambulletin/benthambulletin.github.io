🍂The Turning🍂

Still no number for the three counties. The spotters out this far have not logged enough change to call it, which is where the third week of September usually sits.
Anyone who was raking in Dunkirk yesterday has a better read than the state does. First thing to go is the sumac on the shoulders, then the crowns of the red maples along the creek roads. Numbers as soon as the spotters file them; North Tonawanda usually peaks the last week of October.
