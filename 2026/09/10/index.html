Trump Bans a List of Canadian Goods, Widens the Tariffs

Some alcohol, dairy and motorcycles are barred from entering the U.S. starting September 29, and 50 percent tariffs now cover golf carts, mattresses and more cheese. Road salt came off the list.

2

Republicans Hold Their First Midterm Convention

In Dallas, Trump promised a $5,000 “dividend” if the party keeps Congress and said the Iran war would end right after the election. Several prominent Republicans stayed home.

Iran Referred to the U.N. Security Council

The IAEA reported Tehran for failing to cooperate with an investigation into uranium traces at undeclared sites. Iran called the resolution a fabrication.
