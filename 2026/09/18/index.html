🏈The Gridiron🏈

Bills 41, Lions 31
It was 21–0 before Detroit scored and 27–7 at the half. Josh Allen threw for 248 and three touchdowns — 43 yards to Josh Palmer, one to Dawson Knox, sixteen to Dalton Kincaid — and ran for two more, including a scramble to put it away in the third. Buffalo ran 36 times for 203 yards at five and a half a carry, held the ball for nearly 34 minutes, and did not turn it over.
Jared Goff threw four touchdowns and still lost by ten, which tells you where the game was decided: four sacks, eleven hits on the quarterback, and a Detroit run game held to 66 yards. The penalties the paper flagged Monday came down from ten to five. Buffalo is 2–0 and does not play again until the Chargers come in on the 27th.
The Jets Are 1–0, and Green Bay Is Hurt
New York opened by running it thirty-nine times for 152 yards and holding the ball thirty-nine minutes at Tennessee. Breece Hall went over a hundred; Geno Smith was not sacked once and looked, in one Packers writer's words, more like his Seattle self than his Las Vegas one. Will McDonald had five pressures in twenty-five snaps.
Sunday, 1:00 at the Meadowlands. Hall and rookie tight end Kenyon Sadiq practiced in full Thursday. Four are week-to-week and missed again — Minkah Fitzpatrick, Omar Cooper Jr., Kene Nwangwu and Joseph Ossai — and McDonald was a surprise addition to the report. Green Bay arrives 0–1 and banged up: three interior linemen hurt, two in concussion protocol, Micah Parsons still out with the knee, and Josh Jacobs away from the team. The Packers are favored anyway, which is the part worth watching.
