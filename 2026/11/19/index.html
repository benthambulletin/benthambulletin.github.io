The News
The Lead

Trump, Xi and Putin in One Room

President Trump, China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin sat down together Thursday in Shenzhen, China, a rare meeting of the leaders of three of the world's most powerful countries, CBS reports. Each made a short opening statement, and then they talked behind closed doors for about an hour and a half.

What they said in there is not known. None of the three spoke to reporters afterward, there was no joint statement, and Trump left straight for the airport to fly home.

Before the three-way session, Trump met each of the others alone. With Putin, Russia's state news agency TASS said, the main subject was the war in Ukraine. With Xi, the meeting ran about an hour, and Xi opened by calling relations between China and the United States stable.

"I think a lot of wonderful things can come out of this, or at least start."
— President Trump, standing beside Putin

The National Wire

1

The Biggest Stock Debut Ever Belongs to an A.I. Company

Anthropic, a five-year-old artificial intelligence company, raised more than $81.3 billion by selling shares to the public for the first time, at $83 apiece, NPR reports; that tops the $75 billion SpaceX raised earlier this year. The shares begin trading today on the Nasdaq exchange under the symbol ANT. (A disclosure: this paper is put together with Anthropic's A.I.)

2

Waymo Takes Its Robotaxis Off the Freeway

Waymo has paused driverless rides on freeways across the United States after California banned them there over safety concerns, the BBC reports. The state's motor vehicle department cited a series of incidents, including robotaxis driving into active construction zones.

The Home Wire

1

Rochester · Hochul Lays Out a Nuclear Plan

Gov. Kathy Hochul was in Rochester on Wednesday to unveil a plan for adding nuclear power plants upstate, WXXI reports; the state calls it the Advanced Nuclear Master Plan. She called nuclear power "the most sustainable, the most reliable, and ... the most affordable way we can power our state."

2

Erie County · Road Salt Has Doubled in Price

The salt Erie County buys for its roads has gone from about $43 a ton in 2022 to more than $93 a ton this year, WKBW reports. Highway departments are stocking up anyway after an unusually mild start to November.

3

Chautauqua County · An Aerospace Plant Grows

The county's Industrial Development Agency approved financial help for a $5.22 million expansion by Rand Aerospace & Defense Group, which is buying the former Serta mattress factory in the town of Ellicott, the Observer reports. The second site is expected to keep Rand's 115 jobs and add 31 over three years.

Ella Langley's Night at the CMAs

Ella Langley won entertainer of the year, the top prize, at the 60th Country Music Association Awards in Nashville on Wednesday, and won every category she was nominated in, Page Six reports. "This isn't real life," she said from the stage.

2

A Van Gogh Sells for $190.4 Million

Sotheby's sold Vincent van Gogh's Chestnut Trees in Blossom in New York on Wednesday for $190.4 million, a record for the artist, the BBC reports; his old record was $117.2 million. The sale went ahead after a last-minute agreement settled a claim that the painting had been sold under duress during the rise of the Nazis.
