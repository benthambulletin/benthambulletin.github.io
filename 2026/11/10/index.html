The National Wire

The Lead

Gas Drops Below $4

The national average for a gallon of regular fell to about $3.98 on Tuesday, the first time it has been under $4 in nearly four months, AAA says, CBS reports. It was $4.56 earlier this year.

The fall is mostly seasonal: from Sept. 15, stations may sell a cheaper "winter blend" of gasoline, and people drive less once vacation season ends. Patrick De Haan of GasBuddy, which tracks pump prices, expects prices to keep easing into Thanksgiving travel.

Update: The FBI and Nvidia's Boss

President Trump said the FBI is looking into the affair of Jensen Huang, head of AI chip giant Nvidia, with a woman U.S. agencies suspect was a Chinese spy, the BBC reports. Trump said Huang told him, "I had no idea she was a spy," and that he believes him; China calls the allegations "entirely groundless."

4

Ukraine Talks Resume

American, Ukrainian and Russian negotiators began two days of talks in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday aimed at ending the war Russia started in 2022, the first such meeting since February, CBS reports. They will focus on security guarantees, promises of protection Ukraine wants against a future attack.

The Home Wire

1

New York · Regents Exams Stay Until 2030

The Board of Regents pushed back the end of the state's Regents exams to 2030, WXXI reports. Students will still take one each in English, math, science and social studies, but the tests won't decide whether they graduate.

2

Dunkirk · Pomfret Water Brings Money

Homes and businesses in Pomfret east and south of the Routes 20 and 60 roundabout moved off Fredonia's troubled water system on Friday and now get Dunkirk's water through the North County Water District, the Observer reports. "That will be extra income for the city of Dunkirk," Public Works Director Randy Woodbury said.

Billy Joel Plans a Return

Billy Joel will answer students' questions and play "a little music" at Florida Atlantic University on Dec. 14, his first official appearance since he was diagnosed with normal pressure hydrocephalus, a brain disorder, and left the stage last year, Rolling Stone reports. Tickets go only to enrolled students.

2

Johnny Depp's Scrooge

Early reactions to Ebenezer, Johnny Depp's take on A Christmas Carol, are mostly warm after press screenings and a London premiere Monday night, Variety reports, with critics calling it a "wickedly fun holiday treat" and a strong comeback for its star. Full reviews come out Thursday, and the film opens in theaters Friday, Nov. 13.

3

Fall Out Boy Hit the Road

Fall Out Boy announced a 36-date 2027 stadium-and-arena tour of Europe and North America and released "ACES," their first new song in more than three years, Billboard reports. The North American leg runs 21 stadium dates from July 13 to Aug. 26, including Toronto's Rogers Stadium and Citi Field in Queens, with The Offspring and Good Charlotte along.

The Gridiron

Bills 23, Vikings 6

Buffalo held the ball for 42 minutes to Minnesota's 17 and wore the Vikings down. Josh Allen threw a 25-yard touchdown pass late in the first half, James Cook carried 31 times for 107 yards and ran one in from 5 in the fourth, and Tyler Bass kicked three field goals, the longest from 52. The defense intercepted two passes and held Minnesota to 178 yards. The Bills are 7–1.

Jets Make a Deadline-Week Trade

Ahead of today's 4 p.m. trade deadline, the Jets sent backup safety Andre Cisco to Detroit for a 2028 sixth-round pick, Gang Green Nation reports. Pass rusher Will McDonald has asked for a trade, though coach Aaron Glenn suggested the team isn't looking to move him.
