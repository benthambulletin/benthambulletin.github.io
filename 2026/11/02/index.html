✶The National Wire✶

1

Jet Crash in Palm Beach County

A Learjet 60 flying from Austin, Texas, to the Bahamas nose-dived and crashed near homes in Palm Beach County, Florida, Sunday afternoon, killing all nine people aboard, CBS reports. No one on the ground was hurt, Gov. Ron DeSantis said; about 600 people were evacuated from around the site near a mobile home park, and the cause isn't known yet.

2

Sen. Chris Murphy Has a Stroke

Connecticut Democrat Chris Murphy felt numbness in his arm at his son's hockey game Saturday and was found at a local hospital to have had a stroke, his chief of staff said Sunday, CBS reports. He was moved to Johns Hopkins for monitoring; he is awake, alert and responding well, and his office says it is optimistic he will make a full recovery.

3

A Democrat Crosses Over in Michigan

Democratic Rep. Haley Stevens, who narrowly lost August's Senate primary, endorsed Republican Mike Rogers for Michigan's open Senate seat on Sunday, CBS reports. She said the Democratic nominee, Abdul El-Sayed, had told her she was no longer welcome in his campaign; it's one of the races that could decide who controls the Senate.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Greece · Band Brings Home a State Title

The Greece Blue Knights Marching Band came back from the state championships in Syracuse as champions, Rochester First reports. The band draws from all of Greece's secondary schools and won a lower-class state title in 2023.

2

Dunkirk · A Lung Doctor at Brooks-TLC

Brooks-TLC Hospital System has added Dr. Mandeep Sidhu, a specialist in lung disease and critical care, to treat breathing problems for patients across Chautauqua County, the Observer reports. Her work runs from complex lung conditions and long-term breathing illnesses to the sickest patients, who need machines to help them breathe.

3

Lockport · Fatal House Fire

One person died and another was hurt in a fire at a home on Park Avenue early Sunday, the Niagara Gazette reports. Firefighters were called at 3:37 a.m. for flames coming from the kitchen and a resident trapped inside; the cause is under investigation.

★The Marquee★

1

The Black Parade Finally Tops the Chart

My Chemical Romance has its first chart-topping album: The Black Parade jumped from 139th place to the top of the Billboard 200 after its 20th-anniversary deluxe reissue, Billboard reports. The 2006 album peaked at second place the first time around.

2

Wu-Tang at Halftime in Detroit

Wu-Tang Clan will headline the halftime show when the Lions host the Bears on Thanksgiving, Nov. 26, on CBS, Billboard reports. Eminem and his manager, Paul Rosenberg, picked the act; it's the second year the two have chosen the Lions' Thanksgiving performer.

3

A Sketch Becomes a Sitcom

"The Smile Clinic," a digital short from SNL Korea that skewers Korea's cosmetic skin-clinic business, is being turned into a 10-episode half-hour sitcom, Deadline reports. It's the first spin-off ever from one of Saturday Night Live's international versions.
