✶The National Wire✶

1

Update: Democrats Have the House

Democrats won control of the House by flipping more than two dozen Republican-held seats, CBS reports, from the suburbs of the Northeast to parts of South Texas once seen as turning Republican. With the Senate already won, they will control both houses of Congress when it convenes in January.

2

Mississippi, Too Close to Call

Democrat Scott Colom, a district attorney, led Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith by just over 1,100 votes early Thursday, CBS reports. On Wednesday morning, with about 96 percent counted, only a few dozen votes separated them, a stunningly close race in a state as Republican as Mississippi, CBS says.

3

Joshua Wong Gets 10 Years

Joshua Wong, once the face of Hong Kong's pro-democracy protests, was sentenced to 10 years for "colluding with foreign forces" under the national security law China imposed on the city in 2020, the BBC reports. He has already spent six years in jail and was due out in January.

4

Ronaldo Hangs Up the Number 7

Cristiano Ronaldo, 41, is retiring from Portugal's national soccer team, the BBC reports, with 146 goals and 234 appearances for his country, both world records. He walked out of the team's camp in September after being told he wouldn't start a match against Denmark.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Rochester · RTS On Demand Is Ending

The Regional Transit Service will end RTS On Demand, its rideshare-style service, in 2027 because of money trouble, Rochester First reports; "Expenses are rising much faster than our revenues," CEO Miguel Velázquez said. RTS plans to rework service in those areas instead, including expanding some fixed bus routes, WXXI reports.

2

North Tonawanda · A Backstage for the Riviera

The Riviera Theatre is asking the Niagara River Greenway fund for $175,000 toward a 2,300-square-foot backstage and dressing-room addition connected to the main stage, the Niagara Gazette reports. Today's dressing rooms are in the basement, down a steep staircase that limits bigger shows and anyone who has trouble with stairs.

3

Dunkirk · The Wave Stopped Short

State Sen. George Borrello won another term, 68 to 30 percent over Victoria Guite-Williams, and Assemblyman Andrew Molitor beat Thomas Carle 26,485 votes to 15,490, the Observer reports; both Republicans held on while Democrats flipped at least a dozen seats in Albany. Rep. Nick Langworthy's final margin over Aaron Gies of Olean was 59 to 39 percent, and he carried every county in the district.

★The Marquee★

1

Dylan Meets the Pope

Bob Dylan visited Pope Leo XIV at the Vatican Wednesday during a break between concerts in Italy, NPR reports. The pope gave him a rosary, and Dylan gave the pope a copy of his memoir, Chronicles: Volume One.

2

Neeson Hands Aslan to Streep

Liam Neeson, who voiced the lion Aslan in the 2005–10 Narnia films, says there's "nobody better" than Meryl Streep to take the role in Greta Gerwig's Narnia: The Magician's Nephew, Deadline reports, though he told an interviewer the new film was "news to me," Variety adds. It opens in theaters Feb. 12.

3

Spider-Man Pays Sony's Bills

Sony Pictures earned $581 million in the July–September quarter, up from $93 million a year earlier, Deadline reports. The main reason was the box office for Spider-Man: Brand New Day.
