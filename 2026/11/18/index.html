The Lead

North Tonawanda's New Two Miles of Bike Path Are Open

North Tonawanda opened a new two-mile stretch of bike path on Tuesday, a $2.7 million extension nearly a decade in the making, the Niagara Gazette reports. It runs along the Erie Canal between the North Tonawanda Botanical Gardens and Mayor's Park.

The new piece closes a gap. There is now a continuous run of trail from the Botanical Gardens all the way to Niagara Falls, the paper says, and a better connection to the state's trail system.

The National Wire

1

Trump Lands in China for the Three-Way Summit

President Trump arrived Wednesday in Shenzhen, a center of China's tech industry, where Chinese leader Xi Jinping met him on a red carpet ahead of a summit with Xi and Russia's Vladimir Putin, CBS reports. The Kremlin's spokesman said Putin would meet Trump at a reception before the three sit down together, and Trump may also meet North Korea's Kim Jong Un.

2

Alaska's Senate Seat Flips

Former Rep. Mary Peltola, a Democrat, defeated Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan in Alaska, CBS News projects, two weeks after Election Day; that gives Democrats 56 Senate seats to the Republicans' 44. Peltola, 53, is set to become the first Alaska Native elected to the Senate.

3

Update: "Zero Evidence" the Outages Are Linked

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said there is "zero evidence" that the recent failures in air travel, banking and other systems are connected, CBS reports. He said he understood people are uneasy and wanted to be clear that the United States is not under systemic attack.

The Home Wire

1

Rochester · Shovels in at High Falls State Park

A ceremonial groundbreaking is set for this morning for High Falls State Park, 40 acres mostly in the Genesee River gorge in downtown Rochester, WXXI reports. "This park is transformational for downtown Rochester," said Lisa Baron of the nonprofit Greentopia, which expects to help with programming and job training. "But we want it transformational for everybody."

2

Chautauqua County · Natalie Merchant Rides in the Macy's Parade

Jamestown native Natalie Merchant will perform on a float in this year's Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City, the Observer reports. Others on the floats include the Indigo Girls, Blues Traveler, Queen Latifah and Jimmy Fallon with The Roots.

3

Sherman · A Petition to Dissolve the Village

Sherman's village board has received a petition to dissolve the village, and Mayor Colleen Meeder is pushing back on what she calls "false narratives" around it, the Observer reports. The claims she disputes are that the village does not support its fire department and that it is going bankrupt.

The Marquee

1

Glastonbury Names Its Headliners

Harry Styles, Billie Eilish and Sam Fender will headline England's Glastonbury Festival next June, organizers announced Wednesday, Billboard reports. It will be the famous festival's first edition since 2025.

2

Dancing With the Stars Has Its Finalists

Harry Shum Jr. and partner Jenna Johnson were eliminated Tuesday, one week short of the finale, Us Weekly reports. That leaves Ezra Frech, Maura Higgins, Connor Wood, Tatyana Ali and Amber Glenn for the finale, live on ABC next Tuesday at 8.

3

Tonight on TV: Taylor Swift at the CMAs

Taylor Swift returns to the stage at the CMA Awards, country music's annual awards show, tonight, TVLine reports. Also new today: Nicolas Cage plays the football coach and broadcaster John Madden in Madden on Prime Video.

The Gridiron

Bills: A Rookie Guard to Injured Reserve

The Bills put guard Ar'maj Reed-Adams, a sixth-round pick this year, on injured reserve; he had been on the injury report with back and elbow trouble, ProFootballTalk reports. Defensive back Te'Cory Couch, released before the Jets game, is back on the practice squad after clearing waivers, per Buffalo Rumblings.

Jets: The Long View

The Jets are in line for three first-round picks in the 2027 draft, and two of them would land in the top six as the standings sit now, Gang Green Nation reports, using the draft-order site Tankathon. Gang Green Nation's point: that is enough to add several impact players, perhaps a quarterback among them.
