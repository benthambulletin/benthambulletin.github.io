The Lead

Storms Line Up for Thanksgiving Week

A new round of storms is expected to hit both coasts early this week, just as people head out for the holiday, NPR reports. San Francisco's mayor asked people there to skip travel unless it is essential, with extreme high tides and rain forecast from Monday afternoon through Wednesday.

It has already been a wet fall across much of the country. California saw mudslides, waterspouts and flooding this past week, and Florida had coastal flooding and streets under water. NPR traces part of it to a strong El Niño, a warming of the Pacific that shifts weather around the world.

Closer to home, the trouble is timing rather than storms. The weather service has a chance of rain and snow showers here on Thanksgiving Day itself, after 7 a.m., with a high near 43. Anyone flying out of state this week should check their airline before leaving for the airport.

The classic meal for ten; add ham, potatoes and green beans and it’s $81.72. American Farm Bureau Federation volunteers priced it in all 50 states and Puerto Rico the first week of November.

The turkey did it. A 16-pound bird costs $23.68, up about 10 percent, and it is roughly 40 cents of every dollar on the list. The Farm Bureau's economist, Faith Parum, points to strong demand, tight supplies of turkey and higher costs to raise them. Cranberries, milk, rolls and stuffing all went up too. Four things got slightly cheaper: the veggie tray, pie crusts, frozen peas and whipping cream, each by less than 4 percent.

Why the rest crept up. The Farm Bureau names high diesel prices, which make everything cost more to haul, and a shortage of farm workers. Neither shows up as one big jump; they add a little to almost every item.

What it means for us. The Northeast pays the most of any region, $61.57 for the same meal. A bigger menu with ham, potatoes and green beans comes to $81.72. Next to the 2022 record it is still a cheaper year; next to last year, a few dollars more.

Coming down the road. Turkey prices usually ease after the holiday, when stores stop selling birds below cost to bring shoppers in. The survey runs again next November, and it is the number to compare against.

The National Wire

A Most-Wanted Cartel Boss Is Captured

Mexican special forces arrested Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as "Chapo Isidro," a drug lord on the FBI's 10 most wanted list, after a shoot-out in Sinaloa on Friday, the BBC reports. Seven suspects and four Mexican soldiers were killed, and the United States is seeking his extradition on charges of flooding the country with fentanyl and other drugs.

2

Trump Backs a 10 Percent Cap on Credit Card Rates

President Trump said Saturday he would work with the incoming Democratic Congress to cap credit card interest rates, the Associated Press reports in PBS NewsHour. The usual proposal is a 10 percent cap; most cards now charge far more, so it would cut what people pay on balances they carry.

3

The FBI's Crime Database May Have Been Hacked

The FBI is investigating an apparent breach of its National Crime Information Center, the database police across the country use for warrants and background checks, the BBC reports, citing U.S. media. The agency did not comment; asked about it Saturday, the president said he had "just heard" about it.

The Market

S&P 500, daily close through Friday, Nov. 20. Data: S&P Dow Jones Indices.

The S&P 500, the index of 500 big companies that most retirement funds follow, ended the week Friday at 6,910, 11.6 percent below its Oct. 6 high. That keeps it in a correction, Wall Street's word for a drop of 10 percent or more from a peak. Markets reopen Monday.

The Home Wire

1

Lockport · Homelessness Help, Scattered

People working on homelessness in Lockport say the effort is held back by limited federal money, a shortage of affordable housing and agencies that don't coordinate, the Lockport Union-Sun & Journal reports. Niagara County Legislature chair Mike Hill told the paper the county doesn't get federal housing money directly; it flows through the Homeless Alliance of Western New York, which hands it out in competitive grants.

2

Niagara Falls · Fewer Shootings

Shootings with injuries fell 13 percent statewide in the first 10 months of 2026 in the communities in New York's gun-violence program, the Lockport Union-Sun & Journal reports. Niagara Falls police are one of the 29 departments in it, and they reported fewer shootings than at this point last year.

Miley Cyrus Is Married

Miley Cyrus married drummer Maxx Morando on Saturday at a private home in Palm Springs, Page Six reports, after five years together. Her parents, Billy Ray and Tish Cyrus, were there, and the Flaming Lips' Wayne Coyne, a longtime friend, officiated.

E.J. Carroll, a character actor seen on Law & Order and Boardwalk Empire, died after collapsing on a New York City street Thursday on his way to a voiceover job, Us Weekly reports. He was 66, and a cause of death has not been determined.

On This Day

November 22, 1963 — President John F. Kennedy was shot riding in an open car through downtown Dallas, a little after 12:30 in the afternoon. Within the hour Walter Cronkite read the bulletin on CBS that the president had died at 1 p.m. Central time. Vice President Lyndon Johnson took the oath of office aboard Air Force One before it left Dallas. For a generation, "where were you" meant this day.
