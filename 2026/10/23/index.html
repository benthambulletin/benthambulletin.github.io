✶The National Wire✶

1

No More Carriage Horses in New York

The New York City Council voted Thursday to ban horse-drawn carriage rides by 2028 under Romanch's Law, named for Romanch Mahajan, 18, who died in a carriage accident in Central Park in June. Mayor Zohran Mamdani's office says he will sign it; the Transport Workers Union says it will challenge the ban in federal court.

2

A Son Hears His Mother's Voice

Aung San Suu Kyi, Myanmar's ousted leader, spoke by phone with her son, Kim Aris, on Thursday, their first conversation since the 2021 coup put her in detention, NPR reports. Her first question, he said, was about his health.

3

A Flaw Under Most of the Web

Researchers at Wiz, using OpenAI technology, found security flaws in a programming language that helps run about 70 percent of the world's websites, and hacking attempts have risen since, NPR reports. OpenAI called it "one of the most impactful vulnerabilities in recent memory."

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Rochester · Flu Arrives Early

Monroe County health officials say this flu season is running about a month ahead of past years in the Rochester area, Rochester First reports. County Public Health Commissioner Dr. Marielena Velez de Brown is tracking the early rise in cases.

2

Fredonia · Second Thoughts on Flock Cameras

Village trustees in Fredonia are weighing whether to shut down Flock license-plate cameras, objecting that they went up without a board vote, WKBW reports. The cameras record which vehicles pass a spot and when.

3

Wilson · On to the Semifinals

Hayden Fellows scored four goals as top-seeded Wilson beat Silver Creek/Forestville 6–0 in a Section VI Class C boys soccer quarterfinal Thursday, the Observer reports. The 15–1–1 Lakemen host Randolph Monday at 7.

★The Marquee★

1

Ryan Gosling's Spooky Band Returns

Dead Man's Bones, Ryan Gosling's duo with Zach Shields, will release new music Oct. 27, 17 years after its only album, the Hollywood Reporter says. The ghost-and-monster rock band is timing its comeback for Halloween.

2

Prince William, Superfan

The Prince of Wales dropped in on The Graham Norton Show and said he's a "big fan" of Christopher Nolan's movies and the game show The Traitors, Variety reports. The episode airs tonight in Britain.

3

The Notebook Goes to London

The musical version of The Notebook, with songs by Ingrid Michaelson, will open in the West End, Variety reports. It will be the first show at the newly renamed Judi Dench Theatre, formerly the Shaftesbury.
