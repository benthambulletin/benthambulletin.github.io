BREAKING: Hamburg Voters to Decide Fate of Cheeseburger. In a move that has left residents stunned, a proposition has made its way onto the Village of Hamburg ballot that would ban cheese on any hamburger sold within village limits. Supporters of "Proposition Plain Patty" say the town that gave the world the hamburger should honor its "original, unadulterated recipe," with fines of up to $50 per slice. "The hamburger was born here naked, and it should stay that way," said a spokesperson for the Committee for Burger Purity. One diner owner is already stockpiling American singles "in case things go underground." Polls open Tuesday. Cheese lovers are urged to make their voices heard, or forever hold their pickles.

Update: Oleshky Empties Out

The evacuation of the Russian-occupied Ukrainian city of Oleshky is under way, Russian and Ukrainian authorities confirm, with 25 buses allowed to carry up to 750 people out during a four-hour window this morning, the BBC reports. The ceasefire has covered the whole Kherson front since Tuesday so the road out could be cleared of mines; several hundred residents chose to stay.

2

A $6.3 Billion Crypto Hack

A North Korean unit was likely behind a hack that hit more than $6 billion on Binance, the world's biggest cryptocurrency exchange, a U.S. blockchain intelligence firm says, CBS reports. Binance says it froze about $3 billion, suspended withdrawals for a day or two and will cover all losses.

3

Google's AI Eats Its Ads

Google's parent lost about $500 billion in stock market value after quarterly results in which its business chief said its conversational "AI mode" is eating into the money it makes from ads beside ordinary search results, the BBC reports. Revenue still grew 25 percent, to $127.6 billion; the BBC notes millions now ask chatbots such as ChatGPT and Claude instead of searching.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Monroe County · Fewer Kids With Lead

The number of Monroe County children found with elevated lead in their blood fell 27 percent in 2025, from 299 to 217, even as more were tested, county data released Thursday show, WXXI reports. Of 14,556 children tested, 217 topped the county's threshold; health experts say no level of lead is safe.

2

Niagara Falls · Goodyear Plant Closing

The Goodyear plant on 56th Street will close Dec. 30 and lay off 51 of its workers, a state WARN notice confirms, the Niagara Gazette reports. All but five of the plant's workers are on the layoff list, according to the notice, which was posted Monday but dated Oct. 1.

3

Fredonia · On to the Funke Bowl

Fredonia's football team beat Eden/North Collins 39–21 Thursday to reach the Chuck Funke Bowl final in Class C, the Observer reports. That leaves the Hillbillies one win from a sectional title.

Patrick Stewart Dies at 86

Patrick Stewart, Captain Picard on Star Trek: The Next Generation and Professor X in the X-Men films, has died at 86, CBS reports. His friend and co-star Ian McKellen called him "a man to rely on, admire and in my case, love," the Hollywood Reporter reports.

Early reactions to Godzilla Minus Zero from press screenings this week call it another hit, the Hollywood Reporter reports. It opens in North American theaters Nov. 6.
