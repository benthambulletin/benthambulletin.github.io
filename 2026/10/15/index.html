Inflation Jumps to 3.7%

September's consumer price report showed prices up 3.7 percent from a year earlier, higher than expected. Gas prices jumped 9.1 percent from August and made up more than half of the month's rise.

School Roof Collapse in Pakistan

The roof of a small private school in Peshawar collapsed Thursday, killing at least 28 children and a teacher. More than 50 other people were hurt.

3

Earhart Searchers Say They're Close

American researchers believe they may be days away from finding what remains of Amelia Earhart's plane off a remote South Pacific island. She vanished in 1937.

A New Cousin for T. Rex

A fossil dug up in Britain 100 years ago has been identified as a previously unknown relative of T. rex. One researcher said it "definitely would have chased us."
