Update: Four Dead at Canadian Base

Four Canadian service members were killed in the incident that set off a major fire at a military base in Alberta, CBS reports. Prime Minister Mark Carney called it one of the worst peacetime tragedies for the country's military.

Earhart Search Comes Up Empty

Researchers who traveled to a remote Pacific island say the object they hoped was Amelia Earhart's plane is likely a collection of large trees that washed away. The mystery stands.

Alberta Votes on Separation

Albertans go to the polls on whether to hold a vote on leaving Canada. Polling suggests most want to stay, but the debate alone has divided the province.
