Army Report on the D.C. Midair Crash

The Army's review of the January 2025 collision of an Army helicopter and an American Airlines jet over the Potomac, which killed 67, found failures in air traffic control, helicopter routes, training and safety practices, and called for fixes. It was finished in May and released Thursday after pressure from victims' families.
