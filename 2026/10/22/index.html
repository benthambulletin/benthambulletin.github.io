✶The National Wire✶

1

Two Hostages Freed After More Than a Year

Eva Gretzmacher, 76, an Austrian aid worker, and Claudia Abbt, 69, of Switzerland, kidnapped separately in Agadez, Niger, in January and April last year, have been freed and handed to authorities in Benghazi, Libya, the BBC reports. Who held them and how they were released has not been disclosed.

2

A Starter Home Takes Twice as Long

A median-income renter household now needs 16 years to save a down payment and closing costs for a starter home, twice as long as in 2019, a new Center for Responsible Lending analysis finds, NPR reports. Higher prices, mortgage rates above 7 percent and rising homeowners insurance are all part of it.

3

Deadly Attack on a Saudi Airport

Two people were killed and eight hurt in an attack on King Fahd International Airport in Dammam, in eastern Saudi Arabia, Wednesday evening. Authorities didn't say what struck it; Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis have repeatedly targeted the kingdom's airports with drones and missiles.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Honeoye Falls · An Urgent Care, With Pushback

Construction is set to start within a month on a 10,000-square-foot family medicine and urgent care center at 20 North St., run by Canandaigua's F.F. Thompson Hospital, WXXI reports. The nearest urgent care now is about 20 minutes away in Henrietta; some residents object to the developer's request for $750,000 in tax breaks.

2

Arkwright · A Tax Break for Veterans

The town board approved a property tax exemption for military veterans, the Observer reports. Veterans will save $21 to $107 a year on town taxes starting in 2028; Supervisor Brian McAvoy called it "the right thing to do," if more ceremonial than a big savings.

★The Marquee★

1

Christina Aguilera Drops In

Chappell Roan brought out Christina Aguilera at her Los Angeles show Wednesday night. Aguilera sang "Beautiful," then the two dueted on Roan's "Coffee"; Roan said Aguilera "honestly paved the way for me."

2

Back to Black at 20

Mark Ronson led an all-star tribute to Amy Winehouse at London's Roundhouse Wednesday for the 20th anniversary of Back to Black, Billboard reports. It's the Camden venue where she made her final public appearance in 2011.

3

Spotify Hits 306 Million

Spotify added 6 million paying subscribers last quarter to reach 306 million, Variety reports. Monthly users grew to 788 million, and revenue rose 16 percent.
