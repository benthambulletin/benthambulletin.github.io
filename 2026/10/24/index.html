✶The National Wire✶

1

A Truce for One Ukrainian City

The White House says First Lady Melania Trump brokered a ceasefire for Oleshky, a Russian-occupied city short of food, medicine and clean water, so aid can come in and residents can leave. Russia and Ukraine confirmed the deal, the BBC reports, and both say their forces will stop firing Monday night; the White House puts the start of the truce at Thursday, Oct. 29.

2

Fire at the Eagle's Nest

Hitler's mountaintop retreat above Berchtesgaden, Germany, was engulfed in flames Friday night, and police detained a suspect. About 200 emergency workers fought the fire through difficult terrain; no injuries were reported.

3

Births Hit a Record Low

The U.S. birth rate fell to a record low, the latest federal data show, as fewer women in their 20s have children, NPR reports. The CDC says the fertility rate fell 1 percent in 2025, with a 6 percent drop among women 20 to 24, while the rate for women 30 to 45 is rising.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Rochester · Fewer Emergency Vets

Emergency veterinary care is getting harder to find in the Rochester region, and Lollypop Farm says the problem is spreading nationwide, Rochester First reports. Lollypop is back to a full staff, but many clinics and shelters are not.

2

Niagara & Erie · Radiation Search Narrows

Teams have screened 1,094 properties in Niagara and Erie counties since July, and more than 907 owners have been told no further testing is needed, WKBW reports. Forty properties were referred to the EPA, and cleanup could begin at some within weeks.

3

Silver Creek · Senior Housing Opens Soon

Silver Creek Senior Housing, built on the long-empty site of the old Silver Creek School on Main Street, opens in the coming weeks, the Observer reports. Its 54 apartments are for people 62 and older; 113 people applied, so a lottery will pick the tenants.

1

A First Look at Cliff Booth

Quentin Tarantino surprised an AFI Fest crowd Friday night by showing the first 17 minutes of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, the sequel to his Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, Deadline reports. It's the first public look at the film.

2

Drake Turns 40

Drake celebrated with more than 400 guests in New York Friday night, and the Backstreet Boys showed up to sing "I Want It That Way" with him, Billboard reports. He turns 40 today.

3

Jay-Z Brings Out Blue Ivy

Jay-Z brought out his daughter Blue Ivy Carter, DJ Khaled and John Legend at the first of two nights at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles, Variety reports. He brought Brooklyn to L.A. with "Empire State of Mind."
