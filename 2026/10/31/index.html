✶The National Wire✶

1

Patriot Missiles for Ukraine

The State Department approved the sale of 500 Patriot interceptor missiles to Ukraine for $2.1 billion, paid for by Canada and European allies with no U.S. money, CBS reports. President Zelensky warned it could take "several years" for them all to arrive, the BBC reports.

2

Oil Profits Soar Again

ExxonMobil earned $15.4 billion this quarter, more than double a year ago, and the three biggest oil companies made just over $36 billion together, NPR reports. The war in Iran has cut the oil moving out of the Middle East, and Brent crude trades around $113 a barrel.

3

A Deal for Nature

Nearly 200 countries meeting in Armenia agreed early Saturday on the key parts of a new action plan to protect nature, the BBC reports. It builds on the 2022 pledge to "halt and reverse" nature's decline, including protecting 30 percent of land and seas; money is still the sticking point.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Rochester · A Flower on the Bridge

Work began this week on "In Full Bloom," a nearly 25-foot metal sculpture of the city's five-petal mark on the downtown Sister Cities Bridge, WXXI reports. Its center will light up; the city says it will be finished in the coming weeks.

2

Dunkirk · Police Want Parents to Step In

Dunkirk police answered seven incidents in the past month, from trespassing to robbery and assault, all involving kids 13 to 15, WKBW reports. Chief Douglas Swizer says parents need to be involved, and police have charged several parents with endangering the welfare of a child for failing to control them.

3

North Tonawanda · High School Threat Not Credible

North Tonawanda police say a social media threat made by a North Tonawanda High School student is not credible, WIVB reports. The school district called police Friday about the "concerning" post.

★The Marquee★

1

General Hospital Wins Again

General Hospital won best daytime drama at Friday night's Daytime Emmys, and its Genie Francis received the lifetime achievement award, the Hollywood Reporter reports. Beyond the Gates won four, including best actress for Tamara Tunie.

2

Harryween

Harry Styles opened Friday's Madison Square Garden show dressed as Elvis, then changed into the outfit from his 2010 X Factor audition to sing "Isn't She Lovely," Billboard reports. It was night 29 of his 30-show residency.

3

One Last Role for Eric Bauza

Eric Bauza, the voice of Bugs Bunny who died Oct. 25, will make a posthumous debut on The Simpsons, playing two characters, Deadline reports. He had more than 400 credits.
