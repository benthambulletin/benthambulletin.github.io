🍁The Sunday Spotlight🍁

Spotting an AI Scam
Scammers now use AI to copy voices and write convincing messages; the defenses are old-fashioned and they work. An AI wrote this section.
1. The size of it. People reported $893 million in losses to AI-related scams in 2025, across 22,364 complaints, according to the FBI's annual internet crime report, as covered by the security firm Malwarebytes. What it means: that counts only what people reported, so the real number is higher. The FBI says AI is supercharging old tricks: romance scams, fake kidnapping calls, people pretending to be the government.
2. The voice on the phone. The security company McAfee says about three seconds of someone's recorded voice is enough to clone it, and in its survey of 7,000 people, 70 percent weren't confident they could tell a real voice from a copy. What it means for us: a call that sounds exactly like one of the kids or grandkids, saying there's an emergency and money is needed fast, is the classic setup.
3. Three checks that catch it. Hang up and call back on a number you already have, not one the caller gives you. Agree on a family safe word that anyone in real trouble can say. Never pay because someone is rushing you, and never by gift card or cryptocurrency; the FBI flags those as the scammer's favorites.
Coming down the road. The FBI also suggests posting less of your own voice and video publicly, since that's the raw material. If something does happen, call the bank right away and report it at ic3.gov or reportfraud.ftc.gov. A safe word costs nothing; pick one at the next family dinner.

✶The National Wire✶

1

Brazil Votes

Brazilians choose a president today in a close runoff between Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, 80, seeking a fourth term, and Senator Flávio Bolsonaro, 45, the BBC reports. Bolsonaro finished first in the Oct. 4 first round; his father, the former president, is serving a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup.

2

France Protests Again

More than 1,300 people were arrested Saturday as hundreds of thousands protested across France, with tear gas and water cannon in Paris and barricades burning in Marseille, the BBC reports. The protests are over the cost of living and poor school conditions; the BBC says officials largely avoided the rioting and looting of the weekend before.

3

Shooting at a Homecoming

At least 12 people, six of them children, were shot near the football stadium in Tallahassee after Florida A&M's homecoming game Saturday, CBS reports. Police say a fight broke out first; three are seriously hurt, and no one has been arrested.

☑The Ballot☑

Nine Days Out, and Early Voting Is Open
Governor. Kathy Hochul leads Bruce Blakeman by about 12 points. The newest major poll, Quinnipiac (Oct 15–19), has her ahead 55 to 41, down from 19 points in its September poll; Blakeman leads upstate, where all five of our towns are, 52 to 46. What it means: Hochul is still the clear favorite, but the race has tightened a little.
NY-26, North Tonawanda. Democrat Tim Kennedy, the incumbent, against Republican Dennis Hannon. The Cook Political Report rates it solidly Democratic.
NY-23, Orchard Park, Dunkirk and Fredonia. Republican Langworthy, the incumbent, against Democrat Gies.
NY-25, Scottsville. Democrat Morelle, the incumbent, against Republican McIntyre.
Down the ballot in North Tonawanda. State Senate: Ortt (R, inc.) against Arida (D). The city also votes yes or no on an $18 million community center at the Keller Building, paid for by a special property tax of about 84 cents per $1,000 of assessed value before grants. Every town's full list is on the tracker.
The legislature elsewhere. Orchard Park: Assembly, Burke (D, inc.) vs Dickson (R); Gallivan (R) unopposed for State Senate. Scottsville: State Senate, Helming (R, inc.) vs Mills (D); Assembly, Hawley (R, inc.) vs Wolcott (D). Dunkirk and Fredonia: State Senate, Borrello (R, inc.) vs Guite-Williams (D); Assembly, Molitor (R, inc.) vs Carle (D).
Where to vote early. North Tonawanda: Wheatfield Community Center or Wendelville Fire Co. Orchard Park: the Community Activity Center on California Road. Scottsville: Henrietta Library, Gates Presbyterian or any of 15 Monroe County sites. Dunkirk and Fredonia: the County Fairgrounds 4-H Building.
The national read. Democrats are favored to take the House; Cook expects them to gain 12 to 22 seats. The Senate is close, tilting Democratic: they lead narrowly or are tied in all six Republican seats they're chasing, with Iowa and Kansas dead even, and must also hold Michigan and Minnesota. The president's approval is about 22 points underwater in the average.

1

Rochester · A Night for the Duffys

The Boys and Girls Club of Rochester honored Barb and Bob Duffy at its annual gala downtown Saturday, Rochester First reports. The couple has worked on behalf of the community for more than four decades.

2

Fredonia · A Dunkirk Diplomat Remembered

The Fredonia Shakespeare Club opened its season with a talk on Edna "Betty" Boorady, a lawyer and diplomat born in Dunkirk, by Dunkirk Historical Museum curator Katherine Szewczuk, the Observer reports. Boorady, who graduated from St. Mary's Academy in 1938, was the first woman to direct an overseas mission of USAID, the U.S. foreign-aid agency.

Elvis's Owners Sue an AI Company
