✶The National Wire✶

1

Update: Lula Wins a Fourth Term

With every vote counted, Brazil's electoral court says President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva beat Flávio Bolsonaro with 51.8 percent, a lead of nearly 4.4 million votes, CBS reports. Bolsonaro refused to concede and asked for the results to be thrown out after a judge's last-minute decision to keep polls open two extra hours.

2

Serbia's Vučić Concedes

Serbia's longtime leader Aleksandar Vučić admitted defeat Sunday to a student-led protest movement in early (snap) parliamentary elections, and crowds celebrated in Belgrade and other cities, the BBC reports. The movement formed less than two years ago after a railway station disaster blamed on corruption; an independent projection gives it 52.9 percent of the vote.

3

Machado Comes Home

Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado returned home Sunday after more than 10 months in exile, and huge crowds lined her route through Caracas, the BBC reports. She slipped out of the country last December to accept the Nobel Peace Prize; the U.S. seized President Nicolás Maduro in January and backed his deputy, Delcy Rodríguez, as interim president.

4

A 93-Year-Old Turns the Tables

A 93-year-old woman in western Japan got calls from a man posing as a police officer, who said her bank account had been raided and that an officer would come collect her ATM card, CBS reports. She suspected a scam, told her son and the real police, and officers were waiting at her house and arrested him when he showed up.

⛪The Home Wire⛪

1

Rochester · A $16 Million Facelift for the Hyatt

A $16 million renovation to make the downtown Hyatt Regency a top-flight, full-service hotel will start "as soon as possible," WXXI reports. The Riverside closed in 2020 and the Wyndham this summer, and the deputy mayor says the city is about 1,000 hotel rooms short.

2

Silver Creek · A Home Playoff Game

Silver Creek/Forestville piled up 437 yards in a 31–13 win over Franklinville/Ellicottville to lock up the third seed in the Section VI Class D playoffs, the Observer reports. Manny Sanchez ran for 204 yards, and the Black Knights host sixth-seeded Cattaraugus-Little Valley in next weekend's quarterfinals.

★The Marquee★

1

The Voice of Bugs Bunny Dies at 46

Eric Bauza, the Emmy-winning actor who voiced Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck, died Sunday at 46, his manager said, CBS reports. The Los Angeles County medical examiner lists hypertensive cardiovascular disease, heart damage from long-term high blood pressure; he had more than 400 credits, including Porky Pig and Marvin the Martian.

2

Olivia Rodrigo Back on Top

Olivia Rodrigo's you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love is back atop the Billboard 200, its third week on top, Billboard reports. A new remix of "Expectations" with Chappell Roan drove most of the jump; Zach Bryan's new album debuts in second place.

3

Miranda Lambert's Thanksgiving Halftime

Miranda Lambert will headline halftime when the Cowboys host the Eagles on Thanksgiving, Billboard reports. The show kicks off the Salvation Army's Red Kettle campaign each year, and Lambert says this is its 30th anniversary.

⚑The Chase⚑

Larson Wins Talladega
Kyle Larson fell to 11th on the last lap of overtime, then charged back through the tri-oval to win, his first victory at a drafting track in 59 tries, Hendrick Motorsports reports. William Byron was second and Ricky Stenhouse Jr. third. Larson now leads Denny Hamlin by 73 points, Christopher Bell by 74 and Chase Briscoe by 79. Two races left: Martinsville Sunday, then Homestead.
