Russia Frees an American Teacher

Stephen Hubbard, a former teacher jailed in Russia since 2022, has been released, U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff says. President Trump says he is on his way home and that no prisoner swap was required.

Rochester · $20 Million to Take Down the Terrence Building

Rochester City Council voted unanimously Tuesday night to accept state funding to demolish the Terrence Building, Rochester First reports. The building on Elmwood Avenue went up in 1959.
