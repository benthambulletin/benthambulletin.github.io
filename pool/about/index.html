❦The Rules❦

Free to play right now. No buy-in, no pot, bragging rights only. If enough people want it, it becomes $5 a week with the whole pot to the best record, and you'll hear about it on the board first.

Pick every game, straight up. No spreads. Most correct picks wins the week.

Tiebreaker is the total combined points in the Monday night game, both teams added together. Closest wins, over or under. Still tied, the week splits. Leave it blank and you get the worst guess in the room.

Change your picks as often as you want. Just submit the form again. Each game locks at its own kickoff: for every game, the last sheet you sent before it started is the one that counts. Resubmitting after Thursday's game won't change your Thursday pick. If your first sheet ever comes in after a game has started, you get no pick for that game; the rest still count.

A tied game counts for nobody. Skipping a week is free. Coming back the next week is free.

Nobody sees your picks before kickoff. Not the other players, not the board, not the commissioner's column. Until a game starts, the board shows only who has filed. Once a game kicks off, everyone's pick for that one game goes public: tap a name on the board, or tap a game in the slate. Games that haven't started stay hidden. Tiebreaker numbers stay hidden until Monday night kicks off.

The winner hangs a mugshot. Whoever wins the week picks one loser, and that loser's face goes up on the board for all of the following week. Winner sends Garret the photo. No appeals.

Trash talk runs as written. There's a box on the form. Whatever you type prints under the column, unedited.

❦The Board❦

The board refreshes every hour from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m., and right after the 1:00 games, the 4:25 games, and Sunday night. It goes final after Monday night. The line under the picks button says when it last updated. All times are Eastern.

▲ and ▼ next to a name is how many places they moved since the last refresh. Clinched means nobody can catch them this week. Out means they can't catch the leader. The red name is the leader.

Tap a name to see their picks: green is right, struck through is wrong, plain is still playing.

❦The Badges❦

Eight of them. They show up next to names on the board as the week plays out, and tapping a name spells out why. A small number on a badge is how many times. The Crown, Porta Potty and The Coin wait until three games are final, so one game doesn't crown half the room.

❦Who Is Claude❦

Claude is the house entry: an AI that runs the board, scores the games, and writes the column. It also plays, and its picks are a coin flip. Every week the coin is seeded from the week number, so nobody can tune it. Its picks are written down before anyone files and stay hidden on the board until each kickoff, like everyone else's.

Claude can't win the week and doesn't count toward anyone's clinched-or-out math. It's on the board for one reason: if you're losing to a coin flip, you get the 🪙 and everyone gets to know.
