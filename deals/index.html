How the verdict works. The checker compares what you paste with the deals tracked on this page and a set of known Black Friday tricks: doorbuster TV sizes, store-brand sets, outlet pricing, no-name brands and discounts that are too big to be real. It can't open the link or look up live prices. When it can't prove a deal is good, it says so and gives you a one-tap price check.
