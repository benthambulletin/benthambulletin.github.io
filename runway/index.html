    What your data says audited Oct 10, 2026

    • Waking early is the sturdiest signal: on attack mornings you wake about 24 minutes before your usual time, and a third of attack mornings are 25+ minutes early (9% of normal mornings). Bedtime doesn't change.
    • Body Battery the morning before an attack: 64 vs 76 on ordinary mornings (months you were logging). A morning under 70 is a flag, not a verdict.
    • Resting heart rate runs 1.5–2 bpm above your usual for the three days before.
    • Stress two days out: 37 vs 33. Small, but a third of those days scored 40+.
    • Attacks cluster a little, but a recent attack does not raise tomorrow's odds. Days-since is context, not risk.
    • Pressure: no signal, either direction. Still logged for the family; off this page.
